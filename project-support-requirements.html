PARTNER WITH PUI89 · PROJECT-BY-PROJECT NEEDS

What each project needs to move forward

PUI89 welcomes researchers, engineers, universities, companies, public organisations, data providers and responsible supporters. This page makes the current gaps visible so a potential partner can see where their expertise, equipment, data, facilities or funding may be useful.

Independent development initiative · Requirements are subject to review · No existing partnership or resource is implied

How to read this page: These are current development needs and proposed next-step resources, not claims that PUI89 already owns the equipment, has secured datasets, completed integration, or validated physical performance. Contributions are considered only after scope, safety, data rights, cost, ownership and written terms are reviewed.
01 · AGRICULTURE

Coconut Harvest AI

AI-assisted coconut maturity assessment and a future harvesting-system research direction.

What is needed

  • Real coconut images with documented permission and reuse rights, covering young green, mature green, yellow and brown maturity classes
  • Grower and agronomy expertise to define reliable labelling guidance and difficult visual cases
  • Human-reviewed annotations and a repeatable train/validation/test split
  • Access to a suitable orchard for later controlled data collection and field evaluation
  • Camera and mounting advice for consistent image capture; open-source, CPU-first evaluation tools

What support enables next

Build a permissioned image set, document labels, establish a reproducible baseline and report precision, recall and F1 before making performance claims.

Current boundary: 0–30 m is a design challenge, not a validated harvesting capability. No field-proven harvesting robot is claimed.

02 · DISASTER RESPONSE

Extreme Flood Rescue AI

Decision-support research for flood awareness, aerial observation and rescue-scene assessment.

What is needed

  • Permissioned RGB and thermal imagery with capture context, timestamps and ground-truth review
  • Search-and-rescue, flood-response and remote-sensing expertise
  • Controlled access to suitable RGB/thermal cameras or an aerial platform for future experiments
  • Safe test locations, scenario protocols and independent evaluation support
  • Guidance on geolocation uncertainty, visibility limits and human-in-the-loop procedures

What support enables next

Develop a traceable observation dataset and evaluate whether imagery and metadata can support reliable human review in defined scenarios.

Current boundary: existing work includes a metadata-level RGB/thermal observation contract. No drone connection, person detection, sensor fusion, coordinate estimation or field rescue validation is claimed. The 15 m scenario remains simulation/design work.

03 · MULTIMODAL SCREENING

PUI89 Sentinel

Multimodal screening research designed to support trained human review, not replace it.

What is needed

  • Lawfully obtained, permissioned and representative RGB and X-ray datasets, with provenance and usage rights
  • Qualified screening, imaging and domain specialists to define realistic review criteria
  • Documented examples of image quality, occlusion, false alarms and ambiguous cases
  • Controlled evaluation access and independent review of workflow and limitations
  • Where relevant, lawful access to validated chemical-screening outputs from appropriate instruments—not improvised chemical claims

What support enables next

Define a bounded human-review workflow, data governance, evaluation protocol and clear abstention/escalation rules.

Current boundary: no validated narcotics-detection performance or operational screening certification is claimed. RGB, X-ray and chemical methods have distinct limits; human review remains essential.

04 · INDOOR ASSISTANCE

AI HOME

An indoor four-wheel assistant concept for environmental awareness and human-reviewed alerts.

What is needed

  • Suitable real sensors for environmental monitoring, with data sheets and safe integration guidance
  • Prototype chassis, low-speed drive components and battery engineering advice for a later supervised build
  • Home-environment test access with informed consent and privacy controls
  • Robotics, embedded systems, safety and human-factors expertise
  • Independent review of fail-safe behaviour, charging, thermal risks and alert quality

What support enables next

Move from software-only demonstration toward a controlled sensor-connected prototype, with documented tests and human oversight.

Current boundary: software demonstrator work is separate from physical robot validation. No real home-safety performance or autonomous device control is claimed. Design target: 8 kg, maximum 10 kg; not a verified build specification.

05 · OUTDOOR MOBILE ASSISTANCE

AI NOMAD

A distinct compact outdoor/mobile four-wheel assistant concept, separate from indoor AI HOME.

What is needed

  • Outdoor-capable mobile base, wheels, drivetrain and mechanical design expertise
  • Obstacle, terrain and environmental sensors with documented interfaces
  • Battery, power management, enclosure and weather-resistance engineering advice
  • Supervised outdoor test areas and safe mobility test protocols
  • Human-factors input for handle, portability, serviceability and user interaction

What support enables next

Specify a safe low-speed platform and sensor architecture, then validate basic mobility and perception in controlled conditions.

Current boundary: concept and software planning only; no outdoor mobility, weather protection or field performance is validated. Design target: 8–10 kg, maximum 10 kg; not a verified build.

06 · SMART MOBILITY

AI Robot Car

A human-driver-first intelligent vehicle concept sharing selected capabilities with the scooter programme.

What is needed

  • Access to a suitable vehicle or safe test platform, subject to owner permission
  • Automotive-grade sensor and data-interface expertise
  • Road-scene, obstacle, weather and road-condition datasets with lawful usage rights
  • Closed-course or controlled test access and vehicle-safety review
  • Automotive engineering, diagnostics, thermal and battery-risk expertise

What support enables next

Develop and evaluate non-actuating driver-assistance observations and warnings before any wider integration is considered.

Current boundary: no autonomous steering, throttle or braking is claimed. Any road, flood-depth or collision capability requires separate engineering and safety validation.

07 · STEP-THROUGH MOBILITY

EasyRide AI Step-Through Scooter

A practical step-through two-wheel mobility concept with human-controlled vehicle operation.

What is needed

  • Suitable step-through scooter or motorcycle platform and mechanical engineering input
  • Road, obstacle, weather and vehicle-condition sensors with documented interfaces
  • Automotive electrical, battery, thermal and predictive-maintenance expertise
  • Controlled test facilities, rider-safety review and lawful data collection
  • Human-centred design input for mounting, ergonomics, visibility and warning design

What support enables next

Define a safe, non-actuating AI observation and warning architecture that can be evaluated independently of vehicle control.

Current boundary: no autonomous throttle, steering or braking is intended. Payload and flood-wading targets are unverified and must not be treated as proven specifications.

Shared engineering foundation · Not an eighth product

Shared AI Core

The Shared AI Core is common software infrastructure for all seven projects—not a separate product line. It needs architecture review, interface and test contributions, CPU/edge benchmarking, hardware-neutral integration advice, fault-injection testing and reproducible engineering examples. Its purpose is to help systems work responsibly across heterogeneous compute, sensors and power constraints.

No chip manufacturing, proprietary hardware ownership or validated cross-project deployment is claimed.

Ways to contribute

Expertise and review

Offer a scoped technical review, research supervision, domain consultation or independent evaluation.

Data and permissions

Provide lawful data access, licensing guidance, annotation support or domain-ground-truth review.

Equipment and facilities

Offer in-kind loan, donation or supervised access to sensors, platforms, instruments and test sites with clear terms.

Funding and programmes

Share eligible research grants, challenge programmes, institutional support or milestone-based sponsorship. No investment return is offered or promised.
