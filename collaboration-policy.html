Collaboration & Intellectual Property Policy

PUI89 is an independently developed, software-focused AI project. This policy describes the principles governing collaboration, intellectual property, software contributions, confidentiality and external references.

1. Independent Project Status

PUI89 operates as an independent development project. It does not represent itself as a registered company, hardware manufacturer or officially affiliated organization.

2. Scope of Collaboration

Before work begins, any collaboration, technical discussion, code contribution, resource sharing or other external engagement should have a clearly documented scope, responsibilities, deliverables, timeline and applicable conditions.

3. Intellectual Property Ownership

Original software, source code, AI models, algorithms, system architectures, technical documentation, designs and other materials created by PUI89 remain the intellectual property of their respective rights holders, subject to applicable law and third-party rights. PUI89 does not claim ownership of third-party materials or contributions merely because they are discussed, accessed or used in a project.

No transfer of ownership in PUI89-created materials is intended or authorized by this policy. Any proposed transfer or change of ownership requires a separate written agreement signed by the relevant rights holders.

Access to or participation in a PUI89 project does not, by itself, grant ownership of or permission to use private project materials. Any permitted use must be supported by an applicable license or express written authorization.

4. Open-Source Licensing & Third-Party Rights

Publicly released open-source components are governed by their respective licenses. Third-party software, models, datasets, fonts, images and other technical resources must be used in accordance with their applicable licenses, terms and restrictions. Where third-party rights apply, those rights remain with their respective owners.

5. Confidentiality & Data Protection

Confidential information, private source code, credentials, personal data and restricted technical materials must not be disclosed through public repositories or unauthorized communication channels. Use an agreed, appropriately protected channel when sharing information that is not intended for public release.

6. Written Authorization

Use of PUI89's private materials, name, logo, project identity or restricted technical resources beyond permissions expressly provided by an applicable public license requires prior written authorization. Agreements affecting ownership, access rights, responsibilities or formal commitments must be documented in writing by the relevant parties.

7. External References & Partnership Claims

No individual or organization may claim official partnership, sponsorship, endorsement, representation or affiliation with PUI89 without prior written authorization. PUI89 will likewise not use another organization's name or logo, or claim a partnership with it, without appropriate permission.

8. Contributions & Public Disclosure

Technical advice, proposals, discussions and code submissions do not automatically establish employment, agency, ownership, sponsorship or a formal partnership. The ownership and permitted use of external contributions must be clarified with the contributor before incorporation or redistribution. Publication of project information, technical results, source code or confidential materials must respect applicable ownership, licensing and confidentiality requirements.

Policy status: This page describes PUI89's project principles. It is not, by itself, a contributor agreement, assignment, service contract or legal opinion. Specific collaborations and contributions may require separate written agreements. Applicable laws and third-party licenses take precedence where relevant.
