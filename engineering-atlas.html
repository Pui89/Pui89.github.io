How to read this atlas

Implemented — present in codeTested — supported by recorded checksProposed — planned or conceptual

Evidence before claims

Show source, test scope, date and limitations. A passing software test is not proof of physical-world safety.

Fail safely

Keep proposals separate from physical actions. Uncertain or conflicting evidence should trigger review, not overconfident automation.

Recover before replacing

Preserve originals, version history and independent backups. Verify restoration before claiming recoverability.
