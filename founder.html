Independent AI & Robotics Initiative

Founder — PUI89

PUI89 is an independent initiative focused on applied AI, software foundations and robotics-system concepts. It is built around practical problems, careful development and evidence that can be inspected.

Founder and initiative leadership

Founder — PUI89

The Founder establishes PUI89's direction, priorities and decisions, and coordinates the development of its independent AI and robotics portfolio. AI tools, research, specialist knowledge and collaboration support this work.

PUI89 is an Independent AI & Robotics Initiative. It is not presented here as a registered company or manufacturer.

How PUI89 uses the term “AI Engineering”

“AI Engineering” refers to the discipline and process of designing, developing, integrating, testing and evaluating AI systems. It describes the work and technical field—not the Founder's personal job title or a claim of an engineering qualification.

Clear distinction: the public role is Founder — PUI89. The initiative's description is Independent AI & Robotics Initiative.

Development principles

PUI89 distinguishes concepts from implemented software, automated tests, simulations and validated physical capabilities. Project status and performance claims are limited to the evidence available for each product.

The initiative follows an open-source-first, resource-conscious approach and evaluates external technology for provenance, licensing, security, privacy and suitability before adoption.
