How to interpret our evidence

Software verification means code and structured reports passed specified automated checks.

Simulation evidence means a model or rule-based scenario behaved as configured; it is not a physical test.

Experimental evidence requires documented data, repeatable methods, baselines, metrics and independent review where practical.

Field readiness requires suitable hardware, safety engineering, controlled trials, risk assessment and relevant approvals. PUI89 does not claim this status.

Review the source records

The repositories and workflow logs are the primary public records. Reviewers are encouraged to inspect the test definitions, workflow output, dataset provenance and limitations before drawing conclusions.
