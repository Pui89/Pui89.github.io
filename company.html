PUI89

AI · Robotics · Intelligent Systems
Intelligent technology. Responsible engineering. Real-world impact.

PUI89 is an independent, pre-incorporation AI and robotics technology initiative developing evidence-led software, reusable engineering foundations and robotics concepts for meaningful challenges.

Legal status: PUI89 is currently an independent project initiative and is not registered as a legal company. This profile is not an investment offer, a claim of commercial readiness, or a representation of endorsement by any organisation.

Vision

To develop internationally recognised intelligent systems that address practical challenges across agriculture, emergency response and responsible inspection.

Mission

To build modular AI technology and responsible robotics through research, engineering, simulation, repeatable testing, collaboration and real-world validation.

Technology & product portfolio

PUI89 brings together four applied AI and robotics project families. Each repository is public so researchers, startups, technology companies and potential partners can inspect the software, design proposals, tests and limitations directly.

Agriculture Intelligence

Coconut Harvest AI explores computer vision, coconut maturity assessment and assisted harvesting workflows. Physical harvesting capability and model performance require further validation.

Extreme Flood Rescue AI explores environmental awareness and land-and-water rescue-support robotics. The 15-metre depth is a design/simulation target, not a validated operating capability.

Multimodal Inspection

Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI explores sensor-assisted inspection workflows with human review. Chemical identification and operational screening performance are not validated.

HOMEGUARD AI · Home & Nomad

HOMEGUARD AI is a building-condition intelligence software demonstrator. Its proposed robot family has two distinct designs: HOME for indoor buildings and NOMAD for supervised outdoor travel and camping. The current platform is foundation/simulation-only; no deployed robot or real sensor performance is claimed.

Shared technology foundation: PUI89 AI Core

The proposed shared foundation brings together common software interfaces, world-state representation, perception inputs, uncertainty handling, task planning, safety supervision, simulation and automated verification. Capabilities will be labelled according to actual implementation and test evidence.

Perception & world state

Represent observations, sources, timestamps, objects and environmental context.

Planning & safety

Generate reviewable task proposals and apply explicit safety and human-approval gates.

Simulation & verification

Test software behaviours reproducibly before considering hardware integration.

Who PUI89 is built to work with

PUI89 is looking for problem-led conversations with organisations that can help test, validate or responsibly develop these concepts. These are invitations to collaborate, not claims of existing customers, contracts or partnerships.

AI & robotics companies

Explore reusable perception, world-state, uncertainty, safety-supervision and verification components; discuss technical review, integration or co-development.

Agriculture businesses

Define coconut harvesting pain points, operating conditions, data requirements and a measurable first pilot with growers or equipment partners.

Universities & research teams

Collaborate on computer vision, robotics, multimodal sensing, simulation, benchmark design and independent evaluation.

Disaster-response organisations

Review flood-rescue scenarios, human factors, safety requirements and simulation assumptions before any physical prototype is considered.

Inspection & transport operators

Define lawful, privacy-aware inspection workflows where AI supports trained human review rather than making enforcement decisions.

Incubators, grant makers & sponsors

Discuss milestone-based support for validation, licensed data, independent testing, prototype engineering and founder development.

Potential value and measurable impact

Reduce uncertainty

Turn broad ideas into testable requirements, documented assumptions and measurable engineering milestones.

Build reusable capability

Develop shared software and reporting foundations that can support several application areas without claiming one model solves every task.

Validate before scaling

Use controlled datasets, simulation, user feedback and independent review to decide whether a concept deserves further investment.

Cost transparency & operating policy

This public portfolio is maintained using PUI89's existing GitHub repositories and static website. This update adds no paid SEO service, advertising, cloud AI, paid API, subscription, domain purchase or hardware order.

No surprise-spend rule: PUI89 will not start a paid service, enable a paid API, purchase hardware or accept a third-party charge for a project without explicit prior approval of the exact provider, purpose and maximum amount. If a future opportunity requires funding, its budget and terms must be agreed in writing first.

Viewing the portfolio and public repositories does not require payment. PUI89 does not promise investor returns or charge people to access this public information.

Principles

  • Evidence before claims: distinguish implemented, tested, simulated and field-validated capabilities.
  • Human oversight for high-impact or safety-critical decisions.
  • Privacy, security, licensing and data provenance by design.
  • Cost-conscious development and practical use of accessible tools.
  • Collaboration with researchers, domain specialists, customers and responsible partners.

For AI startups, technology companies & research partners

PUI89 is open to technical review, research collaboration, carefully scoped proof-of-concept work, pilot discussions and product-development partnerships. We welcome feedback from AI startups, robotics companies, universities, agricultural organisations, emergency-response specialists and responsible inspection technology teams.

A practical first step is a short technical review of one project, followed by a written scope covering evidence, deliverables, data access, intellectual property, responsibilities, timeline and any approved costs. No partnership, customer, investment or deployment is implied until agreed in writing.
