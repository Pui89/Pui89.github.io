PUI89

AI · Robotics · Intelligent Systems
Intelligent technology. Responsible engineering. Real-world impact.

PUI89 is an independent, pre-incorporation AI and robotics technology initiative developing evidence-led software, reusable engineering foundations and robotics concepts for meaningful challenges.

Legal status: PUI89 is currently an independent project initiative and is not registered as a legal company. This profile is not an investment offer, a claim of commercial readiness, or a representation of endorsement by any organisation.

Vision

To develop internationally recognised intelligent systems for agriculture, responsible inspection, home assistance, mobile assistance and intelligent mobility.

Mission

To build modular AI technology and responsible robotics through research, engineering, simulation, repeatable testing, collaboration and real-world validation.

Technology & product portfolio

PUI89 brings together six active AI and robotics project lines. Each repository is public so startups, technology companies and potential partners can inspect the software, design proposals, tests and limitations directly.

Current portfolio direction: PUI89 is developing six active product concepts across agriculture, inspection robotics, home and mobile assistance, and intelligent mobility. No single product is represented as a validated commercial release; readiness claims remain subject to product-specific evidence and release gates.

Agriculture Intelligence

Coconut Harvest AI explores computer vision, coconut maturity assessment and assisted harvesting workflows. Physical harvesting capability and model performance require further validation.

All-Terrain Robotics

PUI89 Sentinel — All-Terrain Exploration & Inspection Robot is a four-legged quadruped robot concept for supervised exploration and inspection across varied environments, including challenging outdoor areas and authorised industrial or infrastructure sites. “Explore anywhere” is a design ambition, not a validated capability; mobility, access and environmental limits remain unverified.

GitHub repository ↗ · Project overview ↗

PUI89 AI HOME

AI HOME is a separate indoor home-assistant robot concept with a software-first home-safety platform. Certified safety devices remain primary; physical robot and real sensor performance are not validated.

GitHub repository ↗ · Project overview ↗

PUI89 AI NOMAD

PUI89 AI NOMAD is a carry-on suitcase-style mobile AI assistant with an upright luggage body, four spinner caster wheels at the base and a retractable telescopic pull handle. It is intended for supervised luggage carrying and travel assistance, distinct from AI HOME. Physical performance and airline acceptance are not validated.

GitHub repository ↗ · Project overview ↗

PUI89 AI Global SUV

PUI89 AI Global SUV explores human-driver-first vehicle intelligence, combining AI-powered perception, road awareness, hazard detection, environmental understanding and driver advisory assistance. It is an early-stage software and vehicle design concept—not a built, road-tested or certified vehicle.

GitHub repository ↗ · Project overview ↗

PUI89 AI Step-Through Scooter

PUI89 AI Step-Through Scooter explores inclusive, human-centred motorcycle design and advisory AI. It is not a built, rider-tested or road-certified motorcycle.

Who PUI89 is built to work with

PUI89 is looking for problem-led conversations with organisations that can help test, validate or responsibly develop these concepts. These are invitations to collaborate, not claims of existing customers, contracts or partnerships.

AI & robotics companies

Explore reusable perception, world-state, uncertainty, safety-supervision and verification components; discuss technical review, integration or co-development.

Agriculture businesses

Define coconut harvesting pain points, operating conditions, data requirements and a measurable first pilot with growers or equipment partners.

Industry engineering teams

Discuss computer vision, robotics, multimodal sensing, simulation, benchmark design and independent evaluation with relevant technical teams.

Robotics & mobility organisations

Define safe, human-supervised inspection and mobility workflows, operating requirements and measurable evaluation plans before hardware integration.

Inspection & transport operators

Define lawful, privacy-aware inspection workflows where AI supports trained human review rather than making enforcement decisions.

Incubators, grant makers & sponsors

Discuss milestone-based support for validation, licensed data, independent testing, prototype engineering and founder development.

Potential value and measurable impact

Reduce uncertainty

Turn broad ideas into testable requirements, documented assumptions and measurable engineering milestones.

Build reusable capability

Develop shared software and reporting foundations that can support several application areas without claiming one model solves every task.

Validate before scaling

Use controlled datasets, simulation, user feedback and independent review to decide whether a concept deserves further investment.

Current position: early-stage, pre-incorporation initiative. No revenue, funding, customer traction, registered company status or field-validated robot capability is claimed. The next commercial milestone is a narrowly scoped, evidence-producing validation partnership.

Principles

  • Evidence before claims: distinguish implemented, tested, simulated and field-validated capabilities.
  • Human oversight for high-impact or safety-critical decisions.
  • Privacy, security, licensing and data provenance by design.
  • Engagement with technical specialists, domain experts, potential users and responsible industry partners.

For AI startups, technology companies & industry partners

PUI89 is open to technical review, research collaboration, carefully scoped proof-of-concept work, pilot discussions and product-development partnerships. We welcome feedback from AI startups, robotics companies, agricultural businesses, robotics and mobility specialists and responsible inspection technology teams.

A practical first step is a short technical review of one project, followed by a written scope covering evidence, deliverables, data access, intellectual property, responsibilities, timeline and any approved funding and resource commitments. No partnership, customer, investment or deployment is implied until agreed in writing.
