PUI89 · Research & Innovation

Engineering intelligence that can be examined, tested and improved.

PUI89 explores reusable AI and robotics engineering across agriculture, emergency response, multimodal inspection, home safety and intelligent mobility. The research agenda connects shared intelligence infrastructure with project-specific experiments.

Research themes

01 · Foundations

Shared Intelligence & World Models

Structured observations, world-state representation, temporal change, uncertainty and prediction across different operating environments.

02 · Trustworthy AI

Evidence Engineering & Decision Quality

Data provenance, evidence alignment, calibration, conflict handling, abstention, human review and reproducible evaluation.

03 · Perception

Multimodal and Environmental Intelligence

Research into combining visual, spatial and environmental observations while preserving modality-specific limits and uncertainty.

04 · Physical AI

Robotics, Simulation & Digital Twins

Simulation-first robot workflows, navigation interfaces, virtual environments and safe evaluation before any physical deployment.

05 · Prediction

Change, Deterioration & Failure Intelligence

Study of temporal patterns, anomaly signals, predictive maintenance and early warning with traceable supporting evidence.

06 · Human-centred

Human-supervised Autonomous Systems

Explainable recommendations, explicit authority boundaries, safety gates and meaningful human control over consequential actions.

Research and engineering method

  • Define a measurable problem and intended operating conditions.
  • Record data origin, permission, provenance and known limitations.
  • Build a dependency-light baseline before adopting large models.
  • Test normal, degraded, missing, conflicting and adversarial cases.
  • Use simulation and synthetic fixtures where appropriate; label them clearly.
  • Publish test evidence, failures, uncertainty and remaining work.
