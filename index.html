Technology with a purpose

The goal is to turn useful ideas into testable systems, learn from real needs, and build a foundation for a responsible future technology business. We prefer free and open-source tools where practical and avoid spending before a clear need is established.

Flagship project

Coconut Harvest AI explores AI-assisted selective harvesting across four target categories—young green, developing green, mature green, and brown/dry mature—with a long-term design target spanning 0–30 m palm heights. These are intended capabilities, not validated field performance.

01 / Coconut Harvest AI

Four maturity classes. One evidence-led workflow.

The intended system detects fruit, estimates the target category, lets the operator select what to harvest, checks reach/stability/obstacles/tool readiness, requests human approval, and then would cut, capture, lower, and verify the fruit. Target maturity classes: (1) young green, (2) developing green, (3) mature green, and (4) brown/dry mature. Mixed-stage bunches are a scene condition, not a maturity class. The 0–30 m range is a long-term design target, not a proven reach specification. Current work focuses on image review, annotation quality, dataset checks, safety-aware software, and reproducible evaluation; reliable fruit classification and physical harvesting remain unvalidated.

03 / Multimodal-Narcotics-Screening-AI

A robot-dog concept built around safe, human-supervised autonomy.

The interactive lab demonstrates a procedural four-leg gait, synthetic airport crowd, rule-based obstacle yielding, sensor-style visualisation, telemetry, and fault-response scenarios. It is a browser simulation—not a physical robot or a trained AI model.

How we work

Evidence before claims

Tests, documented failures, repeatable measurements, and clear limitations.

Safety and human control

Uncertain outputs need review; physical systems require independent safety controls and verified emergency procedures.

Security and resilience

Plan for software faults, sensor failure, lost communications, unauthorized access, and recovery. No system is claimed to be immune to every threat.

Free-first development

Prefer local and open tools. No paid service, cloud spend, or equipment purchase without explicit review.
