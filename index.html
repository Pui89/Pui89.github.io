Let's solve a real farm problem

We are exploring a focused first product: Coconut Orchard Intelligence, a human-reviewed workflow to organize orchard observations and harvest-planning records. We want to hear from farm operators and agricultural service providers before deciding what to build next.

Designed around the workflow

Start with the current inspection process, its bottlenecks, and the information people need to make decisions—not with AI for its own sake.

Human-reviewed outputs

Flag uncertain observations, record corrections, and keep experimental AI separate from verified results. No physical cutting commands.

Measure practical value

Where a partner and suitable data are available, compare time, review effort, missed observations, and record consistency against an agreed baseline.

Transparent pilot scope

Data permissions, confidentiality, deliverables, cost, and success measures must be agreed in writing before a trial. No performance or savings guarantees.

Safety, security & resilient operation

Every project is being developed with failure handling and misuse resistance in scope. These are engineering requirements and planned safeguards—not a claim that every hazard has already been implemented, independently tested, or eliminated.

Detect faults and fail safely

Monitor software health, sensor validity, power, temperature, communications, and actuator status where supported. On critical faults, stop hazardous actions, enter a defined safe state, alert the operator, and require checks before resuming.

Cybersecurity and malware

Use least privilege, authenticated access, protected secrets, dependency updates, input validation, backups, and auditable logs. Isolate untrusted data and software; define recovery steps for suspected malware or compromise. No system can promise perfect protection.

Physical tampering and hostile conditions

Plan for damaged cables, sensor obstruction, unauthorized access, theft, impact, fire, extreme weather, and loss of power. Protect people first: inhibit cutting or other hazardous actuation, stop movement when safe control cannot be assured, and hand over to a human.

Human control and emergency response

Provide clear status and fault messages, an accessible emergency stop where physical hardware is used, and a documented manual recovery procedure. Never treat an uncertain AI output as authorization to cut, enter a hazard, identify a substance conclusively, or use force.

Project-specific boundaries

Coconut Harvest AI prioritizes harvest-tool inhibition and stable robot stop; Flood Rescue AI prioritizes safe navigation, retreat/stop behavior, and human-supervised rescue decisions; Multimodal Screening AI prioritizes evidence integrity, uncertainty, privacy, and human review.

Verify before deployment

Use documented threat models, fault-injection tests, simulated scenarios, recovery drills, and measurable acceptance criteria. Keep simulation, bench testing, and field evidence clearly separated. Do not deploy a physical system until critical safeguards are verified.

Our engineering approach

A future company must earn trust through evidence, not presentation alone.

Evidence before claims

Automated tests, reproducible benchmarks, documented limitations, and clear distinctions between simulated and measured results.

Safety and human oversight

Failure handling, explicit uncertainty, human review for consequential decisions, and independent safety controls for physical systems.

Open and cost-conscious

Prefer free and open-source tools where suitable. Avoid paid services and hardware purchases unless their costs and need are reviewed first.

Responsible commercialization

Check software and dataset licences, privacy, security, applicable regulations, and customer needs before product or deployment claims.

A responsible path to products

The roadmap is deliberately evidence-led: establish reliable software, measure performance on appropriate data, validate under intended conditions, and only then assess pilot deployments and commercial readiness. No funding, customers, or product certifications are implied by this site.
