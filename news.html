COMPUTATIONAL ENGINEERING · INDUSTRY NEWS

24 March 2026 · LEAP 71 announcement

The Exploration Company licenses LEAP 71’s Noyron RP for next-generation rocket-engine design

LEAP 71 and The Exploration Company announced a five-year, renewable collaboration under which The Exploration Company will license Noyron RP for propulsion-component geometry generation within its internal computational-engineering programme. The announcement describes Noyron RP as combining first-principles physics, engineering logic, production constraints and empirical feedback.

Why it matters: It is an industry example of computational engineering moving into another organisation’s engineering workflow. The announcement also states that designs will undergo the partner’s standard analysis and testing processes.

AI-DESIGNED HARDWARE · TESTING

11 December 2025 · LEAP 71 announcement

LEAP 71 reports hot-fire tests of two 20 kN methalox engines designed with Noyron

LEAP 71 reported hot-fire testing of two different 20 kN rocket-engine designs: a conventional bell-nozzle engine and an aerospike. The company reported that the bell-nozzle engine reached steady state, while the aerospike test encountered start-up transient issues and was limited to a single burn.

Why it matters: The report illustrates an engineering feedback loop in which computational design is followed by manufacturing, physical testing and model refinement. A successful simulation or generated design alone is not equivalent to validated hardware.
