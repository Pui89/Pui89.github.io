PUI89 is an independent AI and robotics development initiative in Thailand. It develops software foundations, practical AI applications and robotic-system concepts, with emphasis on measurable evidence, safety boundaries and responsible engineering.

One reusable engineering foundation

The PUI89 Shared AI Core is intended to provide common data contracts, world-state tracking, uncertainty handling, safety gates, failure reports and reproducible evaluation records across projects, while preserving domain-specific requirements.

Read about the Shared AI Core →

Evidence and status matter. Project concepts, software tests, simulation results and physical validation are different evidence levels. Concept designs and passing unit tests are not proof of real-world robot capability, product certification or commercial deployment.

Research and collaboration

PUI89 welcomes appropriate research collaborators, academic mentors, domain experts and technical contributors. Collaboration is most useful when tied to a verifiable milestone such as dataset review, benchmark design, software evaluation or a supervised feasibility study.
