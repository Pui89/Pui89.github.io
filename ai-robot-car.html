PUI89 AI Robot Car

A safety-first vehicle intelligence research platform for environmental awareness, hazard analysis, vehicle-health monitoring and explainable driver assistance. A human driver remains responsible for every driving action.

Open GitHub repository ↗

Mission & research scope

Develop an evidence-led software foundation that can interpret documented observations, track uncertainty, assess hazards and present explainable advisories for human review.

Working concept architecture

Observation → WorldState → Hazard Analysis → Safety Gate → Human-facing Advisory → Evidence Record

Engineering boundaries

  • Software-first and simulation-first; no physical vehicle, ECU, vehicle-bus or actuator connection.
  • No steering, throttle, braking or propulsion commands. AI output is advisory only.
  • Missing, stale, conflicting or out-of-range inputs must be visible; uncertainty must propagate.
  • No paid APIs, subscriptions, cloud GPUs, paid datasets or metered services.
  • No unsupported claims of autonomous driving, roadworthiness, waterproofing, fire resistance or certified safety.

Current development status

Early-stage software scaffold with research documentation and an initial software-only contract/safety prototype. No functioning car, validated perception model, real-time vehicle integration or certified safety system.
