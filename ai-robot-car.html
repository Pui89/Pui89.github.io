PUI89 AI Global SUV

A medium-size global SUV concept with HEV-first research and powertrain-neutral PUI89 AI. Flood-risk awareness is designed for route avoidance, not flood wading.

Open GitHub repository ↗

Medium-size Global SUV · HEV-first

Preferred first powertrain study: Full Hybrid (HEV). A medium-size, five-door SUV concept. Full HEV is the first powertrain architecture to evaluate and does not require routine external plug-in charging. This is a design direction, not a selected production vehicle or a tested drivetrain.

  • HEV is the first option to evaluate; petrol-only remains a simplicity benchmark.
  • Do not treat mild hybrid (MHEV) as equivalent to full hybrid (HEV).
  • Compare actual Thailand-market vehicles for fuel use, purchase price, warranty, maintenance, parts and service access before a final choice.

One AI Core · Multiple powertrains

PUI89 Core is designed to remain independent of propulsion type and support petrol-only, HEV, PHEV and BEV contexts through versioned, read-only vehicle-data adapters.

  • Reuse Observation, WorldState, ActionProposal and FailureReport.
  • Keep propulsion-specific telemetry in adapters, not separate AI stacks.
  • AI remains advisory: no ECU/CAN, steering, throttle, braking, propulsion, battery-management or charging commands.

Flood resilience · Avoid first

  • Warn about flood-prone roads and recommend route avoidance.
  • Unknown water depth, current or passability means “unknown — do not enter”.
  • No universal safe wading-depth claim; never encourage driving through moving or rising water.
  • After suspected water ingress: do not restart or charge; arrange qualified inspection and recovery.

Fire & thermal-event safety

  • Use supported smoke, temperature and battery/electrical fault signals only; show missing sensor data.
  • If fire, smoke or thermal event is suspected: evacuate, move away and contact emergency services.
  • Do not instruct occupants to fight a vehicle or traction-battery fire.
  • HEV hazard analysis must cover both fuel and high-voltage systems.

Evidence & safety limits

  • AI provides advisory warnings only; no vehicle or emergency-system actuation.
  • No claim of fireproofing, waterproofing, safe flood wading or certification.
  • Software scenarios are not physical safety tests; physical claims require qualified engineering and independent validation.

Mission & research scope

Develop an evidence-led software foundation that can interpret documented observations, track uncertainty, assess hazards and present explainable advisories for human review.

Working concept architecture

Observation → WorldState → Hazard Analysis → Safety Gate → Human-facing Advisory → Evidence Record

Engineering boundaries

  • Software-first and simulation-first; no physical vehicle, ECU, vehicle-bus or actuator connection.
  • No steering, throttle, braking or propulsion commands. AI output is advisory only.
  • Missing, stale, conflicting or out-of-range inputs must be visible; uncertainty must propagate.
  • No unsupported claims of autonomous driving, roadworthiness, waterproofing, fire resistance or certified safety.

Current development status

Early-stage software scaffold with research documentation and an initial software-only contract/safety prototype. No functioning car, validated perception model, real-time vehicle integration or certified safety system.

PUI89 — Seven-Dimension Quality Framework

Last reviewed: 8 November 2026 · Review period: November 2026

Coloured markers identify improvement dimensions, not certification or completed implementation.

  1. ● Brand identity
    Consistent PUI89 naming, typography, Deep Navy/Electric Cyan identity and project accent.
  2. ● Website and accessibility
    Accurate EN/TH parity, responsive pages, accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, clear links and current dates.
  3. ● AI software and applications
    Versioned contracts, traceable evidence, uncertainty handling, safe abstention, human oversight and reproducible tests.
  4. ● Physical products and robotics
    Measurable safety requirements for structure, power, heat, water ingress, fire risk, maintenance and emergency stop; mark untested items honestly.
  5. ● Documentation and research evidence
    Current architecture, requirements, tests, data/model provenance, licences, limitations and evidence links.
  6. ● Product validation and release gates
    Clear user problem, intended operator, value, support, lawful data/IP and validation gates before commercial claims.
  7. ● Professional environment and presentation
    Consistent technical presentations, diagrams, demonstrations and facility requirements without implying unverified partnerships or facilities.
