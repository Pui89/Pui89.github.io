Manufacturing transfer

A second-country factory receives a controlled, revision-matched package: approved CAD and STEP, dimensioned PDF/DXF drawings, released BOM, material/process specifications, assembly and wiring instructions, inspection criteria, calibration, end-of-line tests, maintenance and change-control records. The site must pass first-article inspection and process validation before production approval.

Evidence before claims

Proposed means a concept or candidate. Specified means documented requirements. Tested means a named test was performed and recorded. Verified means the approved acceptance criteria passed with traceable evidence. A target is not a measurement; a simulation is not physical validation; a component datasheet is not system qualification.

The programme-level -50°C to +60°C range is a design objective, not a blanket product rating. No certification, NASA affiliation or production readiness is claimed without supporting evidence.
