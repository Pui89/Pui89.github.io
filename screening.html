03 / PUBLIC-SPACE INSPECTION · AI APPLICATION + ROBOTICS CONCEPT

Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI

A proposed multimodal screening and inspection research project exploring how different evidence sources could support trained human reviewers in controlled, lawful settings.

IN DEVELOPMENTEVIDENCE-LED DESIGNHUMAN OVERSIGHT
Development status · NOT COMPLETE

The project is at software workflow and concept-development stage. No validated narcotics detector, chemical identification, X-ray integration, trained detection dog/robot, field accuracy or autonomous inspection capability is claimed.

01 / Problem & intent

Problem & intent

Multiple evidence types

Visual, sensor and document signals may differ in quality, relevance and reliability.

High consequence of error

False positives can unfairly affect people; uncertainty and independent review are essential.

Responsible inspection

Any use must have lawful authority, privacy safeguards, clear limits and trained human oversight.

02 / System direction

System direction

AI application direction

Explore provenance-aware inputs, modality quality checks, disagreement handling, uncertainty reporting and a human-review queue. A model score is not proof of a substance or offence.

Robotics direction

A mobile quadruped-style inspection concept may be studied for controlled environments. Mobility, sensor payload, obstacle handling, endurance and safe interaction remain unbuilt/unvalidated design questions.

SPECIAL SCOPE / DEVICE SCREENING

E-cigarettes and vaping devices — separate category

Possible visual recognition of a disposable/reusable vape, pod, cartridge, tank, atomiser or refill container is a device-screening task only. The cautious output is “Possible vaping device — human review required.”

A visible device does not establish whether its liquid contains nicotine, THC/cannabis, another controlled substance or none. RGB appearance cannot identify chemical contents. Substance identification requires a separate validated analytical method, calibration, lawful sample handling, independent ground truth and qualified human review. E-cigarettes are not automatically narcotics.

For background, see WHO e-cigarette information, WHO ENDS/ENNDS terminology and Thai Customs. Check current law with qualified counsel before operational use.

Robot identity, modalities & human safeguards

PUI89 Sentinel — proposed mobile inspection assistant

PUI89 Sentinel is the proposed name for a mobile inspection assistant. It is a research concept, not a built or validated robot. The AI role is to analyse permitted inputs, identify visible patterns or sensor anomalies, communicate uncertainty and help trained staff decide whether further inspection should be considered.

Possible modalities — subject to separate validation

  • RGB imagery: visual patterns and scene context, without claiming chemical identification from appearance.
  • X-ray imagery: only when suitable, lawfully obtained and licensed datasets and appropriate inspection equipment are available.
  • Chemical-sensor outputs: only from suitable sensors with documented operating principles, calibration and validated outputs.

Each modality requires its own dataset provenance, ground truth, calibration where applicable, independent evaluation and documented limitations. Multimodal fusion must also be evaluated separately; combining inputs does not automatically make a conclusion reliable.

Safety rule: An AI alert is not proof of narcotics or wrongdoing. The system must not autonomously accuse, detain, search, seize property or make enforcement decisions. Consequential actions require authorised human review and applicable lawful procedures.

03 / What is not finished

What is not finished

This project is still in development. It is not a finished product.

  • No validated class-specific narcotics detection or chemical identification performance.
  • RGB appearance alone cannot establish chemical composition or substance identity.
  • No confirmed X-ray, spectrometry or other analytical sensor integration or calibration.
  • No physical quadruped robot, autonomous public-space inspection or operational deployment has been validated.
  • AI must not independently accuse, search, detain, confiscate or make enforcement decisions.

05 / Standards & governance

Standards & governance

AI, privacy and governance references

Use the NIST AI Risk Management Framework as a lifecycle reference. Consider applicable privacy and data-protection law, public-sector procurement rules, evidence handling and human-rights safeguards in each deployment jurisdiction. Machinery/robot safety standards must be selected for the actual robot design by qualified specialists.

These references do not certify the project. Device recognition, substance identification and legal determination are separate tasks and require separate evidence.

E-cigarettes/vaping devices are a separate screening category. A visible vape device is not automatically narcotics, and its appearance cannot identify liquid contents. See the dedicated device-scope section and WHO e-cigarette information.

06 / HOW THE ROBOT WORKS

PUI89 Sentinel — supervised workflow

01 · AuthoriseOperator defines the permitted area, purpose, route and data limits.
02 · Move safelyBounded low-speed movement; yield, stop or safe-hold on a safety fault.
03 · Check evidenceConfigured modalities are checked independently for source, time, quality and calibration.
04 · ReviewUncertainty or disagreement creates a neutral human-review task, not an accusation.

Robot architecture

  • Four-legged mobility concept with protected central chassis.
  • Raised 360° awareness mast and forward RGB/depth camera positions, if fitted.
  • Sealed modular payload bay for future validated instruments only.
  • Emergency stop, operator console, event log and supervised safe-hold.

Evidence-aware AI

  • Track source, timestamp, quality and calibration state.
  • Keep each modality separate until independently validated.
  • Mark missing or conflicting evidence as UNKNOWN / CONFLICT.
  • Never infer chemical identity from RGB appearance alone.

Human authority and safety boundary

AI may support a neutral review queue. It must not autonomously accuse, detain, search, seize property or make enforcement decisions. No facial recognition, biometric identification or person profiling. A robot alert is not proof of narcotics or wrongdoing.
