PUI89 SENTINEL · PROPOSED RESEARCH SCOPE

E-cigarettes & vaping-device screening

A distinct inspection category within the Multimodal Screening AI project. E-cigarettes are not automatically narcotics, and a device's appearance cannot establish what its liquid contains.

Current status: proposed scope only. No validated vape-device detector, nicotine/THC identifier, chemical-sensing integration or field performance is claimed.

1. What may be in scope

  • Disposable and reusable e-cigarettes, vape pens and device-like objects.
  • Pods, cartridges, tanks, atomisers and refill containers.
  • E-liquids, visible batteries, chargers, packaging, labels and accessories.
  • Visible damage or tampering cues, only where a lawful inspection and suitable validated method exist.

2. Separate device recognition from substance identification

Computer vision may be evaluated for flagging a possible e-cigarette or vaping device. It cannot determine from RGB appearance whether a liquid contains nicotine, THC/cannabis, another controlled substance, or none of these. A vape device must not be labelled “narcotics detected” simply because it is a vape.

If chemical contents need identification, that is a separate analytical task requiring suitable validated instrumentation, documented calibration, lawful sample handling, independent ground truth and qualified human review.

3. Human-review output

Suggested cautious output: “Possible vaping device — human review required.”

Uncertain, conflicting or out-of-scope observations must be referred to an authorised reviewer. AI must not independently accuse, search, detain, confiscate or make enforcement decisions.

4. Legal, privacy and evidence safeguards

  • Rules differ by jurisdiction and change over time. Obtain a current legal review for the deployment location; this page is not legal advice.
  • For Thailand, official government and customs information describes restrictions concerning e-cigarette imports and related products. Verify current official rules before operational use.
  • Respect privacy, non-discrimination, lawful authority, evidence provenance and chain-of-custody.
  • Do not treat reference photographs as training data without checking source, licence, attribution, retrieval date and file hash.

5. Reference sources

  • WHO — Tobacco: E-cigarettes
  • WHO — ENDS/ENNDS terminology
  • Thai Customs Department
