PUI89 · PARTNERSHIPS & TECHNICAL COLLABORATION

Partnerships & Collaboration

PUI89 is an independent, founder-led Industrial AI initiative—but independence does not mean working alone. We welcome partnerships with companies, hardware manufacturers, relevant startups and experienced professionals who can contribute expertise, technology, hardware, resources and practical support to help turn product concepts into real-world solutions. The founder retains strategic direction and leadership.

1. Who we may work with

01 · COMPANIES

Technology companies

Companies with relevant AI, robotics, embedded systems, sensing, computing or product-engineering capabilities.

02 · HARDWARE

Hardware developers

Manufacturers and developers proposing compatible processors, accelerators, sensors, cameras, edge computers or robotics components for technical evaluation.

03 · STARTUPS

Relevant startups

Small teams with a concrete technology or product capability that addresses a defined PUI89 engineering need.

04 · PROFESSIONALS

Experienced specialists

Individual AI developers, software engineers, electronics and embedded engineers, robotics specialists and product engineers with relevant experience.

2. A small, focused operating model

PUI89 is currently developed by one founder. The intended core team is deliberately small—one founder and, only when justified, one or two specialist collaborators. The six product concepts belong to one PUI89 portfolio and will be developed progressively; they are not all being built simultaneously.

PUI89 welcomes partnerships that create practical value. They may be project-specific or ongoing, depending on mutual interests, technical fit, available capacity and agreed goals. The founder retains strategic direction and leadership, while the core team remains deliberately small. A partnership does not automatically imply co-ownership, a joint venture or a large team.

3. Propose a hardware evaluation

Companies and hardware developers are welcome to propose hardware support, including equipment, components, technical resources and access to suitable platforms for evaluation. A proposal should identify the exact model and specifications, intended use, access period, software or driver requirements, safety constraints, ownership and return arrangements, and any costs.

Evaluation depends on technical suitability, available capacity, resources, safety and mutually agreed written conditions. A proposal does not guarantee acceptance, testing, endorsement or product integration.

4. Clear terms before work begins

  • Agree in writing on scope, deliverables, responsibilities, timeline and review criteria.
  • Clarify fees, expenses, equipment ownership, shipping, insurance and return arrangements.
  • Define intellectual property, licences, confidentiality, data rights and publication permissions.
  • Use organisation names, logos or partnership claims only with appropriate written permission.
  • Do not share confidential, personal or restricted information through public issues or email without an agreed secure process.

An enquiry, discussion, hardware proposal or code contribution does not create an employment relationship, agency, endorsement, sponsorship or formal partnership. PUI89 is an independent project initiative and does not claim registered-company status.

5. Contact

Please include your company or professional background, relevant expertise, the specific product or engineering need, what you propose to contribute, and any initial conditions.
