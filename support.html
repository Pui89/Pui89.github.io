PUI89 · SUPPORT

Support PUI89

PUI89 is an independent AI and robotics initiative from Thailand. We welcome suitable support that helps advance research, software and evidence-led engineering.

1. Support PUI89

PUI89 is currently a founder-led, software-first development initiative. The immediate need is practical support that helps turn documented software foundations into testable systems. Physical prototypes and field performance are not yet established.

01 · EXPERTISE

Technical guidance & mentoring

AI, computer vision, embedded software, electronics, mechanical engineering, robotics, product design, safety and independent code review.

02 · HARDWARE

Equipment & test access

Suitable cameras, sensors, edge computers, development boards, robotics platforms, measurement tools and supervised access to test facilities.

03 · COMPONENTS

Materials & components

Electronics, motors, batteries, controllers, mechanical parts, fabrication materials and prototyping supplies, subject to compatibility and safety review.

04 · RESEARCH

Data, research & collaboration

Properly licensed datasets, research access, independent testing, academic or industry collaboration and open-source contributions.

Financial sponsorship and in-kind contributions are welcome as possibilities, not assumed or required. Please discuss scope, ownership, costs, delivery and conditions before sending money, equipment or materials. Do not ship items before arrangements are agreed.

2. Collaboration

We welcome research and technical collaboration in AI and robotics, mentoring, code review, independent testing, open-source development and knowledge exchange with individuals, universities, laboratories and organisations.

An enquiry or proposal does not mean that funding, a supporter or a partnership has been confirmed.

3. Trust & Safety

  • Official channels: PUI89 website and GitHub.
  • Never share passwords, one-time passcodes, recovery codes, private keys or API tokens with anyone claiming to represent PUI89.
  • Verify senders and organisations independently. Be cautious of guaranteed funding or investment offers that demand upfront fees.
  • Agree in writing on scope, costs, data rights, intellectual property and working conditions before work begins.
  • Do not publish personal information, restricted datasets or confidential project information in public spaces.

PUI89 is an independent initiative. This page is not a securities or investment offer, does not promise returns and does not claim registered-company status.

To propose support or discuss collaboration, please include the area of interest, proposed contribution, scope and any initial conditions.
