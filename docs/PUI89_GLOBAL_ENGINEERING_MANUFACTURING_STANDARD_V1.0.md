# PUI89 Global Engineering & Manufacturing Standard v1.0

**Status:** Public engineering framework and preliminary product design index  
**Revision:** 22 November 2026  
**Scope:** Six active PUI89 products; Shared AI Core is common infrastructure, not a seventh product.

## Mission

Make every PUI89 product understandable, manufacturable, inspectable, maintainable and transferable across qualified international engineering and manufacturing teams. A competent engineer must be able to identify the current design, distinguish confirmed facts from proposals, find every open decision, reproduce the released configuration and verify quality without relying on undocumented founder knowledge.

## Three documentation levels

1. **Public Website and README:** product identity, intended users, verified status, approved design targets, high-level dimensions, candidate component/material families, interfaces, limitations, and links to engineering briefs.
2. **Controlled Manufacturing Partner Package:** released CAD, drawings, exact BOM, grades, tolerances, work instructions, wiring, inspection criteria, calibration, end-of-line tests and approved alternatives. Share with qualified partners under appropriate access and confidentiality controls.
3. **Internal Engineering Evidence:** detailed calculations, safety factors, risk register, prototype failure records, supplier evaluations, security-sensitive information and full test history.

Do not confuse public transparency with publishing every confidential or safety-sensitive engineering record.

## Global measurement and documentation rules

- SI units are authoritative: mm, kg, N, N·m, W, Wh, V, A, °C.
- Every drawing includes product/part ID, revision, date, units, datums, tolerances, material, finish and inspection method.
- Every requirement has a unique ID, rationale, verification method, acceptance criteria, owner and status.
- Use consistent terminology and controlled English as the primary engineering language; provide Thai translations where useful. Add local-language manuals and labels for destination markets.
- Keep CAD source plus neutral STEP; 2D PDF plus DXF; BOM in structured CSV/XLSX; wiring diagrams in editable source plus PDF; test records in controlled revision.
- Every released component has an exact manufacturer/model, specification, revision, country of manufacture, provenance, approved alternatives and lifecycle status.
- No substitutions based only on visual similarity or nominal dimensions.

## Materials and components

Public briefs may identify candidate material families and component classes for engineering discussion. They are not an approved BOM. Exact grade, temper, heat treatment, thickness, coating, process, supplier, component model, ratings and tolerance are released only after load analysis, environmental qualification, lifecycle review, sourcing verification and design approval.

A second source or second country is acceptable only after form/fit/function, safety, EMC, thermal, durability, calibration and system-level equivalence are verified.

## Extreme-climate programme

The programme-level study envelope is -50°C to +60°C. It is a design objective, not a blanket operating rating or product claim. For every product and subsystem, separately specify operating, storage, transport, charging and survival limits. Batteries, displays, lubricants, seals, sensors, cables, tyres, actuators and electronics have different limits and require subsystem-specific qualification.

Evaluate cold start/soak, heat soak and solar load, thermal cycling, humidity/condensation, dust/sand, water ingress, corrosion, vibration, shock and transport loads according to actual product use. Define product-specific test severities and acceptance criteria. IP ratings do not automatically establish resistance to immersion, chemicals, salt, pressure washing or extreme temperatures.

## Global manufacturing transfer

Before a second-country factory is authorised, provide a revision-matched controlled package:
1. Approved 3D CAD and neutral STEP.
2. Dimensioned 2D PDF/DXF drawings and GD&T as required.
3. Released BOM and approved-source/alternative register.
4. Exact material and process specifications.
5. Assembly work instructions, fixtures and special-process controls.
6. Electrical schematics, harness drawings, connector pinouts and software release identifiers.
7. Inspection plan, gauges, calibration and acceptance criteria.
8. End-of-line functional and safety tests.
9. Packaging, transport and storage instructions.
10. Maintenance, repair and spare-parts information.
11. Non-conformance, deviation and engineering-change process.
12. First-article inspection and manufacturing-site qualification.

A factory transfer is not approved merely because drawings were sent. First-article inspection, process validation, quality-system review and product verification are required.

## International market readiness

Create a separate compliance matrix for each product and target country. Confirm exact product classification, applicable laws/standards, conformity assessment route, test laboratory, importer/manufacturer obligations, language, labelling, radio/electrical/vehicle rules and technical-file requirements. EU, US and Japan requirements are not interchangeable. Do not claim CE, FCC, PSE, UL, ISO, NASA or other approval without evidence for the exact product and scope.

## Evidence labels

- **Proposed:** concept or candidate design.
- **Specified:** requirements or engineering definition documented.
- **Tested:** a named test was performed; report conditions and results.
- **Verified:** requirements passed against approved acceptance criteria with traceable evidence.

A target is not a measurement. A simulation is not physical validation. A component datasheet is not system qualification. No production-readiness claim is permitted before the required release gates pass.

## Release gates

Requirements freeze → preliminary design review → detailed design review → prototype configuration → verification and validation → manufacturing validation / first article → market-specific conformity and release approval.

**PUI89 principle:** No country dependency. No engineer dependency. No unverified design.
