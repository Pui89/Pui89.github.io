# PUI89 — Partner and Funding Brief

**AI engineering, applied research and robotics concepts**

Website: https://pui89.github.io/  
GitHub: https://github.com/Pui89

## Who we are

PUI89 is an independent, evidence-led AI and robotics initiative developing reusable engineering foundations and applied concepts across agriculture, disaster response and public-safety screening.

PUI89 combines three complementary activities:

- AI robotics systems and mission concepts.
- AI engineering and reusable technology.
- Applied research, prototyping and product development.

PUI89 is in development. This brief does not represent a claim that the initiative is an incorporated company, that physical robots have been built, or that any AI system is validated for operational use.

## Shared technical programme: PUI89 AI Core

PUI89 AI Core is the proposed common foundation for:

- Evidence-linked system status and reproducible test reports.
- World-state and event tracking.
- Uncertainty handling for missing, stale, noisy and conflicting inputs.
- Failure reporting, conservative fallback and human oversight.
- Data provenance, licensing and evaluation traceability.
- Simulation-first evaluation and readiness gates.

The first deliverable is a shared machine-readable status-report schema and portfolio baseline. Subsequent work will implement automated validation in the three project repositories.

## Applied programmes

### Coconut Harvest AI
Agricultural perception, coconut maturity assessment and harvesting-workflow research, with a proposed robotic harvesting platform. Initial collaboration needs: agricultural domain experts, lawful image datasets, orchard access for future evaluation, and robotics/mechatronics advice.

### Extreme Flood Rescue AI
Flood-scene understanding and rescue-support robotics concepts. Initial collaboration needs: disaster-response experts, hydrology and fluid-mechanics advice, rescue-service input, and simulation expertise.

### Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI
A multimodal screening workflow concept with uncertainty reporting and authorised human review. Initial collaboration needs: qualified sensing researchers, lawful and ethically governed evaluation data, measurement expertise and public-safety governance input.

## What PUI89 is seeking

1. **Research partners** — universities, laboratories and domain specialists to co-design testable work packages.
2. **Pilot partners** — organisations willing to define a controlled problem, provide authorised access and evaluate a prototype when ready.
3. **Technical mentors** — software assurance, robotics, perception, data governance and product engineering.
4. **Funding partners** — grant programmes or co-funding organisations supporting a defined R&D work package.

## Proposed first collaboration work package

**Title:** Evidence-led PUI89 AI Core foundation and cross-project evaluation.

**Indicative duration:** 12–16 weeks, subject to partner agreement and resources.

**Deliverables:**
- Versioned shared status-report schema.
- Automated schema and evidence-integrity validator.
- Compatible project reports in all three repositories.
- Reproducible test and failure-report format.
- Portfolio evidence dashboard specification.
- Partner-reviewed next-stage research plan and budget.

**Success measures:**
- All three project reports validate against the same schema.
- Each reported result links to a commit, run or traceable source.
- Failed and partial tests are represented, not hidden.
- Proposed, simulated and tested capabilities remain distinguishable.
- No unsupported model-accuracy, hardware-readiness or safety claims.

## Funding readiness and eligibility

PUI89's legal status, applicant identity, ownership, IP rights, co-funding capacity and ability to receive grant funds must be confirmed before any application. Some Thai programmes require an eligible Thai juristic person, minimum Thai shareholding, prior commercial activity, matching funds or a partner. Do not assume eligibility from the existence of a project repository.

Potential Thai routes to investigate include:

- National Innovation Agency (NIA) thematic innovation support, including AI/robotics and agriculture-related themes. Published NIA guidance describes matching grants of up to 75% and up to THB 5 million for qualifying projects; the current call, applicant rules, eligible costs and matching contribution must be checked.
- NIA Corporate Co-funding, a recoverable-grant mechanism with corporate/private funding participation; this is not a simple unconditional grant.
- Thailand Science Park / NSTDA partnership and landing programmes for market access, research capabilities and ecosystem connections. Programme rounds and eligibility vary.
- depa Digital Startup Fund and relevant AI/digital programmes, subject to current calls and legal-entity requirements.

These are leads for due diligence, not confirmed open awards or a statement that PUI89 currently qualifies.

## Evidence and transparency

The public repositories record historical rule-based software/scenario checks. These are not model accuracy results, validated physical simulations, field trials, robot performance demonstrations or safety certifications. Every funding proposal should identify the precise evidence available at submission date.
