# PUI89 Development Stage Standard

**Purpose:** communicate the documented development state of each PUI89 product without presenting a commercial-readiness percentage, maturity score, certification, or unverified performance claim.

## Five-stage framework

| Stage | Name | Evidence required to enter the stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Concept and requirements | Problem, intended users, scope, constraints, initial requirements and key risks are documented. |
| 2 | Architecture and software foundation | Architecture, interfaces, software contracts or an initial software/workflow foundation are documented. Physical capability is not implied. |
| 3 | Integrated software prototype and simulation | A repeatable end-to-end software prototype or simulation exists, with defined inputs, outputs, tests and recorded limitations. |
| 4 | Controlled integration and validation | Relevant components are integrated and tested against documented acceptance criteria in a controlled environment. |
| 5 | Field validation and demonstrated performance | Representative real-world trials, measured outcomes, safety controls and limitations are documented and independently reviewable. |

These are evidence-based project-stage classifications, not percentage scores. A stage advances only when its entry evidence is available and reviewable. The same stage does not imply that different projects have identical capabilities.

## Reporting rules

- Display **Development Stage** and **Evidence-based development stage** consistently in English.
- Display **ขั้นการพัฒนา** and **ระยะการพัฒนาตามหลักฐาน** consistently in Thai.
- Show the current stage, a short project-specific focus, review date, evidence, limitations and next milestone.
- Do not display commercial-readiness percentages, overall maturity scores or implied completion percentages as substitutes for evidence.
- Commercial, legal, safety and release criteria may remain as explicit validation gates; they are not a development-stage score.
- Distinguish concept, software implementation, simulation, controlled physical testing and field evidence.
- Do not claim a physical prototype, certified function, field performance or product availability without direct evidence.

**Review basis:** current public project documentation and evidence available in the project repositories. Review dates remain those shown on the individual project records.
