## Purpose

PUI89 AI Robotics is an independent AI and robotics initiative developing practical software and prototype workflows for agriculture, disaster response, and human-reviewed multimodal inspection. We want to turn ambitious ideas into testable components, examine failure cases, and publish clear records of what works, what fails, and what remains unproven.

## Three applied projects

- **Coconut Harvest AI:** computer-vision and orchard-workflow research. The priority is a trustworthy, rights-cleared image dataset and a reproducible, held-out benchmark. A reliable coconut detector and physical harvesting robot are not yet validated.
- **Extreme Flood Rescue AI:** simulation-first planning and fault-response research across aerial, surface-water, and ground/amphibious platform concepts. A 0–15 m scenario value is simulated, not a physical vehicle depth rating.
- **Multimodal Narcotics Screening AI:** human-reviewed inspection workflow research. Incomplete or conflicting inputs should trigger uncertainty or review; ordinary imagery is not proof of chemical identity and does not authorize enforcement.

## Immediate milestone opportunities

1. Review coconut-image permissions and labels; create a leakage-aware held-out set and publish measured baseline performance when the dataset gate permits it.
2. Improve reproducible simulation tests for stale sensors, communication loss, uncertainty, safe hold, and recovery.
3. Discuss one real agricultural workflow with domain experts and define how its value could be measured.
4. Progress toward one compatible, lightweight simulator integration only after the Python scenario suite is passing.

## How to support

We welcome technical mentorship, open-source contributions, agricultural domain expertise, permissioned data, research collaboration, introductions to grants or industry partners, suitable in-kind resources, and carefully scoped sponsorship. Support can begin with a focused code review or an introductory conversation; no commitment is assumed.

## What partners can expect

- Clear scope and proposed acceptance criteria before supported work begins.
- Public, reviewable code and technical progress where sharing is appropriate.
- Honest separation of implementation, tests, simulation, measured benchmarks, and field validation.
- Explicit discussion of data rights, privacy, licences, costs, deliverables, ownership, and reporting before any formal arrangement.
- Recognition only with permission and in accordance with agreed terms.
