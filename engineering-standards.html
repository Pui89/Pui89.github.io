PUI89 Engineering Governance

Evidence-Based Engineering & Product Readiness

A system is only as ready as its evidence. PUI89 uses controlled maturity levels and explicit evidence gates to keep public claims aligned with what has actually been specified, implemented, tested, and validated.

No Evidence, No Readiness Claim.

Six sequential maturity levels

01 · Proposed

An idea or candidate approach. The problem, intended use and concept status are identified.

02 · Specified

Scope, requirements, interfaces, acceptance criteria, assumptions, risks and exclusions are documented.

03 · Implemented

A working implementation exists with a version-pinned source or build and reproducible execution instructions.

04 · Tested

Defined tests have been run with recorded environment, inputs, results, failures and reproducible logs.

05 · Validated

Evidence demonstrates fitness for the intended use under defined conditions against pre-approved acceptance criteria.

06 · Product-Ready

Applicable product-level engineering, safety, reliability, usability, legal/conformity and release obligations have been completed and approved.

Levels are sequential. A product or capability cannot skip a level. Code, a passing CI run, a demo, or a simulation alone does not establish validation or product readiness.

Evidence status is reported separately

COMPLETE · Required evidence for the current gate is present and reviewed.

PARTIAL · Evidence exists, but required items are missing.

BLOCKED · A known issue, risk, rights restriction or failed criterion prevents progression.

NOT ASSESSED · No adequate assessment has been performed.

EXPIRED / SUPERSEDED · Evidence no longer applies to the current version or operating conditions.

Product-Ready is market- and release-specific

Product-Ready is not a universal certificate. Each release must identify its product version, intended markets, applicable standards and regulations, assessment route, exclusions and approval record. Internal tests, independent laboratory tests, accredited certification and legal market authorisation are distinct forms of evidence.

Public policy summary

This page is the public-facing summary of PUI89's evidence-based engineering and product-readiness policy. It describes the maturity levels, evidence states and release principles used in public portfolio reporting.

Adoption status: proposed for portfolio-wide adoption. Each product and capability must be assessed independently. Publishing this policy does not establish that any PUI89 product is Validated or Product-Ready.
