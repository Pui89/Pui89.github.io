Early-stage R&D · Research and design, not a hardware release

What PUI89 is building

PUI89 is an independent, early-stage AI and robotics R&D initiative. Current work includes software foundations, architecture, engineering concepts, research, simulation and documented verification. This page describes candidate technologies—not a claim that robots have been manufactured, integrated, certified or commercially released.

Shared engineering foundation

Reusable compute, sensing, control interfaces, power architecture, mechanical standards, safety systems and evidence records.

Project-specific engineering

Each robot receives its own materials, load cases, environmental limits, preliminary BOM, safety analysis and validation plan.

Evidence before claims

Research is not acquisition. Acquisition is not integration. Integration is not validation.

Responsible sourcing

Prioritise open documentation, repairability, verified origin, licensing, security, availability and cost transparency.

Component evidence and development gates

Research & shortlist

Record manufacturer, exact model, origin, datasheet, licence, interface, dimensions, electrical limits and availability.

Design & compatibility

Check fit, load, power, thermal limits, environment, cybersecurity and project compatibility.

Acquire & integrate

Only after explicit approval and budget review; record exact revision and configuration.

Test & validate

Record method, environment, sample size, results, failures, limitations and evidence.

Status: RESEARCHED → SHORTLISTED → DESIGNED → ACQUIRED → INTEGRATED → TESTED. Advance only with evidence.

No purchase, subscription, paid API or cloud commitment is implied. Physical movement and safety-critical functions require independent safeguards, professional review and human approval. Concept art and simulations are not physical test results.
