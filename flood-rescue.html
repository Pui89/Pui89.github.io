02 / DISASTER RESPONSE · AI APPLICATION + ROBOTICS CONCEPT

Flood Rescue AI

A developing AI decision-support concept for flood situational awareness, hazard mapping and human-supervised assistance planning, with proposed land-and-water rescue platforms.

IN DEVELOPMENTEVIDENCE-LED DESIGNHUMAN OVERSIGHT
Development status · NOT COMPLETE

Software checks and scenario/report tooling are under development. The 0–15 m flood range is a scenario/design target only. No validated flood-depth operating envelope, navigation, buoyancy, payload, rescue or field performance is claimed.

01 / Problem & intent

Problem & intent

Disrupted access

Flooding can isolate communities, block roads and damage communications.

Changing hazards

Current, debris, water depth, visibility and structural damage can change quickly.

Responder safety

Technology must support trained rescue teams, not create new hazards or replace their judgement.

02 / System direction

System direction

AI application direction

Combine permitted visual observations, map context and environmental inputs; track freshness and uncertainty; produce reviewable hazard summaries and assistance priorities for trained responders.

Rescue robotics direction

Explore suitable rescue-platform concepts for different flood environments. Detailed configurations and engineering parameters are not published; physical performance remains unvalidated.

Mission approach

Coordinated, human-supervised rescue research

The project explores AI-assisted flood-scene understanding, hazard awareness, route proposals and evidence reporting for trained responders. Specific platform configurations, operating envelopes and unreleased engineering parameters are intentionally not included in this public overview.

Validation boundary: simulations support software research only. Physical navigation, flotation, water resistance, carrying capacity and rescue performance have not been validated.

03 / What is not finished

What is not finished

This project is still in development. It is not a finished product.

  • No validated flood perception, water-depth estimation, SLAM or autonomous navigation performance.
  • 0–15 m describes a research/simulation scenario range, not a submergence rating or guaranteed operating depth.
  • No built or validated amphibious robot, buoyancy, stability, propulsion, payload or people/animal transport capability.
  • Simulation and software tests do not prove real flood rescue effectiveness.

Development sequence

05 / Standards & governance

Standards & governance

Safety and responsible deployment

Use ISO 12100 as a machinery risk-assessment reference where applicable and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework for AI risk management. Applicable marine, electrical, radio, occupational and emergency-response requirements must be identified by qualified specialists for the actual platform and jurisdiction.

These are references, not certification. No person or animal should be carried in an experimental platform before independent engineering review and staged safety validation.
