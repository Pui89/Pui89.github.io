02 / AI STEP-THROUGH SCOOTER

PUI89 AI Step-Through Scooter

Two-wheel AI Mobility · Step-through Design

A premium two-wheel, step-through AI scooter motorcycle concept for two-person everyday mobility, with a continuous flat footboard, upright seating, enclosed drivetrain, under-seat storage and shared PUI89 AI safety intelligence.

  • 190 kg proposed total payload including riders and cargo; engineering validation required
  • Eight AI areas: road hazards, flood routes, weather, collision risk, battery/thermal health, maintenance, SOS and road-memory navigation
  • Deep Navy + Electric Cyan; large under-seat storage; smooth protective side covers
  • Advisory-only AI; no autonomous throttle, steering or braking
  • Concept only: no physical scooter, payload/speed validation, flood/fire test or road certification
