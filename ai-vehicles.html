02 / AI MOTORCYCLE

PUI89 AI Robot Motorcycle

Rider Risk Intelligence · Simulation-first

A rider-assistance research concept covering road hazards, surface and traction context, vehicle-state monitoring, and evidence-linked warnings.

  • 15 synthetic rider-risk and traction scenarios in the initial simulation foundation
  • Advisory-only outputs; no throttle, brake, steering or ignition commands
  • Thresholds are illustrative test fixtures, not validated safety limits
  • No physical motorcycle or road-use certification

PUI89 engineering principles

◆ No paid APIs, subscriptions, metered services or cloud GPUs

◆ No connection to physical vehicles, motors, brakes, steering, ECUs or batteries

◆ Missing, stale, conflicting inputs and uncertainty must be explicit

◆ Clearly distinguish concepts, implemented software, simulation results and independent validation
