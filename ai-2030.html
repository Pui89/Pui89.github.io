AI for the Real World

PUI89 focuses on AI that can perceive multiple forms of information, operate within real device constraints and connect with physical systems—developed through evidence and testing.

Editorial update: 19 November 2026Global coveragePhysical AI · Multimodal · Edge Compute

This page summarises strategic direction. It does not claim that future products, robots or capabilities have already been built or validated.

Three focus areas

Only the technology directions directly connected to PUI89's engineering goals.

01 · Physical AI & Robotics

AI connected to the physical world

Connect perception, environmental understanding and task planning with robots and real devices. Simulation, testing and appropriate safety gates are required before deployment.

02 · Multimodal AI

Understand more than one type of information

Combine images, audio, text and sensor inputs where appropriate to improve contextual understanding, while representing uncertainty and abstaining when evidence is insufficient.

03 · Efficient AI & Edge Compute

AI designed for real resource limits

Match models and workloads to CPUs, GPUs, NPUs, MCUs, memory, sensors and available power. Measure performance on target devices before making claims.

Engineering principle

From ideas to systems that can be verified

PUI89 prioritises evidence, safety, privacy, human oversight and testing in real environments. Proposed, Specified, Implemented, Tested and Validated states must remain clearly distinguished.

This is a development direction—not a claim that physical capabilities or safety performance are ready for use.
