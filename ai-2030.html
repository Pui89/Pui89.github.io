Strategic vision · 2026–2030

From Intelligent Software
to Physical AI Products.

PUI89 is developing an evidence-led, software-first Industrial AI Platform that connects perception, governed AI workflows, heterogeneous edge computing, simulation and seven domain-specific product lines.

This is a strategic direction and staged roadmap—not a claim that future agents, robots, vehicle systems or product capabilities already exist.

North star

Useful AI in the physical world—bounded, efficient, inspectable and accountable.

PUI89 does not aim to train the largest foundation model or manufacture chips. It aims to make AI work reliably under real constraints: evidence, uncertainty, compute, energy, privacy, safety and human authority.

Six global shifts translated into PUI89 priorities

01 · Agentic AI

Governed AI agents

Bounded workflows, allowlisted tools, scoped permissions, human approval, resumable tasks and auditable reports—not unrestricted autonomous operation.

02 · Work transformation

Human–AI collaboration

Assist people with inspection, analysis, maintenance and decision support. Keep consequential decisions and physical actions under appropriate human authority.

03 · Physical AI

Perception connected to action

Combine vision, multimodal sensing, spatial understanding, task planning, simulation and product-specific safety gates for robots and mobility systems.

04 · Scientific AI

Domain-specific intelligence

Use multimodal and evidence-aware methods where justified. Healthcare and drug discovery are global trends, not PUI89 product claims or current portfolio scope.

05 · Compute & energy

Heterogeneous AI efficiency

Match workload and model size to CPU, GPU, NPU, DSP, MCU, memory, sensors and power. Measure performance and energy before claiming efficiency gains.

06 · Governance

Trust, safety and evidence

Preserve provenance, timestamps, uncertainty and failure records; support abstention, safe hold, least privilege and reviewable human approvals.

One platform. Seven specialised applications.

PUI89 applies one shared Industrial AI Platform across seven specialised product-development tracks. Each project has its own users, engineering requirements, milestones and validation gates while reusing common intelligence and engineering capabilities where appropriate.

From strategy to product portfolio

One reusable foundation. Seven distinct real-world applications.

The platform connects perception, task intelligence, multimodal sensing, compute orchestration, simulation, reliability and evidence. Product-specific designs and safety boundaries remain separate; shared technology does not imply that every capability is already implemented or validated.

2026–2030 execution sequence

2026–2027

Foundation & proof

Stabilise Shared AI Core, PTI, evidence contracts, permissions, CPU-first tests and measurable product requirements.

2027–2028

Demonstrable workflows

Build bounded product workflows, evaluate on held-out data, and measure quality, latency, memory and energy where possible.

2028–2029

Supervised Physical AI

Progress through simulation, bench testing, controlled prototypes and supervised trials with explicit safety gates.

2029–2030

Productisation readiness

Assess reliability, maintainability, privacy, cybersecurity, sourcing, lifecycle cost and regulatory requirements.

Engineering rules that do not change

  • Distinguish proposed, specified, implemented, tested, simulated, validated and certified.
  • A model output or ActionProposal is not permission to execute.
  • No paid APIs, subscriptions or cloud GPU services without explicit approval.
  • No automatic downloads, installations, purchases or supplier commitments.
  • No physical capability, safety, accuracy or energy-saving claim without appropriate evidence.
  • Use simulation as a pre-test tool, never as proof of field performance.
