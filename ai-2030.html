AI trends shaping the road to 2030

Six broad themes help organise the fast-moving AI landscape. Their pace and real-world impact will differ by sector and region.

01 · Agentic AI

From chatbots to task-oriented agents

AI systems are moving toward multi-step workflows, tool use and coordination across applications. Reliability, permissions, security and human oversight remain central adoption challenges.

02 · Multimodal & reasoning

Models that work across different kinds of information

Text, images, audio, video and spatial inputs are increasingly combined. The next challenge is dependable reasoning across modalities, not simply accepting more input types.

03 · Physical AI & robotics

AI moves from screens into the physical world

Robotics, autonomous machines and intelligent devices connect perception with planning and action. Simulation, safety engineering and real-world validation are essential before deployment.

04 · Efficient AI & compute

Smaller models, specialised chips and inference economics

Model compression, quantisation, specialised accelerators and heterogeneous computing are making efficiency, latency, memory and energy major design considerations.

05 · AI for science & engineering

AI becomes a research and discovery tool

AI is being applied to scientific discovery, materials, engineering design and complex simulations. Generated results still require domain expertise and experimental verification.

06 · Trust, security & governance

Provenance, cybersecurity and accountable systems

As AI gains access to tools and workflows, organisations must address data provenance, content authenticity, model security, privacy, evaluation and human accountability.
