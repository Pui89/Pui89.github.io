# PUI89 Brand Guidelines

## Brand architecture
- **Master brand:** PUI89.
- **Descriptor:** Intelligent Systems, AI & Robotics (descriptive positioning, not a claim of incorporation or market leadership).
- **Brand promise:** build useful intelligent systems through careful engineering, measurable evidence and responsible development.
- **Voice:** clear, technically credible, calm, practical, transparent and internationally understandable.

## Logo
Use the approved PUI89 logo assets already maintained in the repository. Preserve the bold N symbol, cut-end geometry and original proportions. Do not redraw, stretch, skew, add a roof/house/shield motif, or change the mark per project. Use the approved light, dark or monochrome variant with adequate contrast. Keep clear space at least the width of one main N stroke around the mark. Do not add glow effects that reduce legibility at small sizes.

## Master palette
- Deep Navy: #071426
- Electric Cyan: #00D9FF
- Technology Blue: #176BCE
- Teal: #00AFA5
- Research Green: #43B581
- Silver: #C5D0DC
- White: #FFFFFF
- Ink: #142235

Electric Cyan is an accent, not a body-text colour on white. Use accessible dark text for small text. Project palettes are defined in project-identities.md.

## Visual language
Use clean geometry, purposeful whitespace, subtle technical grids, precise line icons, restrained gradients and authentic product/research imagery. Avoid excessive neon, decorative circuit-board clichés, cluttered collages, tiny text and generic stock imagery that could be mistaken for a real PUI89 prototype.

## Imagery and truth
Every image must have a clear purpose and accurate caption/alt text. Label generated or illustrative hardware as “Concept visual” or “Illustrative concept” where relevant. Do not imply a prototype, test, deployment, certification or customer exists unless documented evidence supports it.

## Naming
Use canonical project names consistently. Product/project accent colours never replace the PUI89 master identity. Use the current canonical names from the project README and website inventory; do not silently revive legacy names.

## Voice examples
Prefer: “Indoor mobile-assistance robot concept; prototype development planned.”  
Avoid: “World-leading autonomous home robot” unless independently substantiated.
