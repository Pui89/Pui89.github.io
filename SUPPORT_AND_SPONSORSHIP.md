## Why support this work?

PUI89 is developing practical AI and robotics prototypes with an emphasis on transparent engineering, reproducible evaluation, responsible use, and cost-conscious development. The first flagship is **Coconut Harvest AI**, exploring human-reviewed orchard image inspection and harvest-planning support. Other concepts address flood reconnaissance and multimodal screening.

The projects are in development. Support helps move them from software prototypes toward better evidence, useful workflows, and carefully scoped real-world evaluation. This page does not imply existing sponsors, customers, grants, field validation, or institutional partnerships.

## Measurable milestones

See the [Support Milestones plan](SPONSOR_MILESTONES.md) for specific proposed work packages, useful contributions, and acceptance evidence. These milestones are proposals, not claims of completed results or guaranteed deliverables.

## What support would make the biggest difference?

### 1. Technical mentorship and research collaboration
Help review computer vision evaluation, dataset quality, software architecture, ROS 2/Gazebo simulation, safety cases, or reproducibility. Focused code reviews and constructive technical feedback are valuable contributions.

### 2. Agricultural data and workflow partners
Connect us with coconut growers, agricultural officers, cooperatives, or researchers willing to discuss orchard inspection needs. Any images or records must have documented permission and suitable privacy and reuse terms before use.

### 3. A carefully scoped pilot partner
Help define one measurable farm workflow. A pilot should begin with discovery and offline/human-reviewed software—not autonomous cutting. Scope, access, data permissions, safety, responsibilities, costs, and success criteria must be agreed in writing before any trial.

### 4. In-kind equipment or compute support
Potential support includes access to suitable cameras, depth sensors, test equipment, robotics lab time, or compute credits. We prefer existing or donated resources and local/offline testing. No purchase or paid service will be initiated without explicit approval.

### 5. Grants, fellowships, and research sponsorship
We welcome introductions to relevant open-source, agricultural innovation, responsible AI, robotics, and research funding opportunities. Eligibility, application requirements, allowable costs, and any reporting obligations would be checked before applying.

### 6. Open-source contributions
Review documentation, reproduce tests, improve usability/accessibility, contribute code, or help check dataset licences and annotations. Start with the repositories and their contribution guidance.

## What an initial contribution can fund or enable

We will prioritise measurable, low-cost milestones rather than broad promises:

1. **Data readiness:** document image provenance and permissions, review labels, and remove unsuitable or duplicate samples.
2. **Reproducible evaluation:** run a baseline on a reviewed held-out set and publish metrics, runtime context, and failure cases.
3. **Usable prototype:** improve local setup, input validation, demo workflow, and user documentation.
4. **Simulation and safety:** document robot geometry assumptions and test conservative plans in simulation before considering physical hardware.
5. **Partner discovery:** record a specific workflow problem and agree on measurable evaluation criteria with a potential partner.

These are intended milestones, not guaranteed deliverables. Timelines depend on available data, resources, and collaborator commitments.

## Accountability and boundaries

- **Free-first by default:** target additional spend is USD 0 unless the project owner explicitly approves an exception.
- **No exaggerated claims:** prototype code, synthetic examples, test results, and field-validated outcomes will be described separately.
- **Clear scope:** any funded work should have written goals, a budget, deliverables, timelines, ownership/licensing terms, and a reporting plan.
- **Responsible data use:** do not send confidential farm records, personal data, or sensitive images through public GitHub issues or unsolicited email.
- **Safety first:** no prototype should directly control cutters or actuators. Physical trials require appropriate supervision, risk assessment, emergency stop, exclusion zones, and independent safety controls.
- **No implied benefits:** sponsorship does not guarantee investment returns, product performance, exclusivity, or commercial availability.
- **Recognition by permission:** sponsor names, logos, and acknowledgements will be published only with written permission and in accordance with any agreement.
