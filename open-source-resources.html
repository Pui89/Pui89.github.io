PUI89 · TECHNOLOGY & ENGINEERING LIBRARY

Open-Source Technology Stack & Engineering Resources

A practical reference for the technologies that may support PUI89 products and the shared AI engineering platform — from perception and edge inference to robotics, mobility, agriculture, simulation and verification.

PUI89 Home

How to read this library: This is a curated evaluation reference, not a claim that every tool is adopted or implemented. Software licences do not automatically cover model weights, datasets, hosted services or commercial deployment. Verify the exact version, licence, provider, provenance, hardware requirements, privacy terms and intended use before adoption. Chinese-origin model families and unclear-provenance resources are excluded in line with PUI89's current sourcing preference.

Adoption status

Selected — preferred candidate for a defined task Evaluating — requires comparison and validation Future research — not in the current implementation scope

A resource appearing on this page is not automatically selected. Status must be assigned per project, version and use case after review. No tool is represented here as installed, tested or validated unless supporting evidence is published separately.

1 · AI software & data foundations

Selected candidates

  • PyTorch — model training and inference.
  • OpenCV — image and video processing; check the exact version and licence.
  • scikit-learn — classical ML and evaluation.
  • NumPy, pandas and Jupyter — data analysis and experiments.

Use small, reproducible CPU baselines before considering larger models or hardware.

2 · Multimodal AI & local inference

Evaluating

  • Hugging Face Transformers — model tooling; inspect each repository and model card.
  • llama.cpp — local inference runtime.
  • Ollama — local model runner; model terms remain separate.
  • Model families for individual review: Google Gemma, Microsoft Phi, Meta Llama and Mistral.

Check model-weight terms, commercial rights, download size, RAM/VRAM, quantization and offline operation. Do not assume that an open-source runtime makes its model weights open-source.

3 · Vision, perception & datasets

Selected candidates Evaluating

  • OpenCV — preprocessing, geometry and classical vision.
  • CVAT — image/video annotation; verify edition and deployment terms.
  • FiftyOne — dataset inspection and error analysis.
  • PyTorch — detection, classification, segmentation and tracking model workflows.

Prioritize licensed real-world evidence, human-reviewed labels, dataset provenance, leakage-resistant splits and uncertainty reporting.

4 · Agentic AI & workflow orchestration

Future research

  • PydanticAI — typed agent workflows.
  • LangGraph — stateful orchestration.
  • smolagents — lightweight agent experiments.
  • CrewAI — multi-agent workflows.
  • OpenAI Agents SDK — agent SDK; model/API inference may require a paid provider.

Agents are optional advisory tools only. They must not directly control steering, throttle, braking, propulsion, ECU/CAN or remote vehicle functions. Hosted services and metered inference are not assumed to be acceptable.

5 · Edge AI & compute efficiency

Evaluating

  • ONNX Runtime — portable inference runtime.
  • TensorFlow Lite — on-device inference tooling (review current project direction and support).
  • OpenVINO — model optimization and inference for supported hardware.

Shared AI Core focus: model sizing, quantization, scheduling across CPU/GPU/NPU/MCU, memory and power budgets, latency measurement, offline-first operation and safe fallback. Hardware compatibility must be verified before selection.

6 · Robotics & autonomy research

Evaluating

  • ROS 2 Jazzy — robotics middleware.
  • Gazebo — robot simulation.
  • MuJoCo — physics simulation.

Use for research, software integration and simulation. Simulation results do not establish physical safety or field capability.

7 · Mobility & traffic simulation

Future research

  • CARLA — autonomous-driving research simulator.
  • SUMO — traffic and mobility simulation.

Potential relevance to PUI89 AI Global SUV and AI Step-Through Scooter for scenario replay, hazard analysis and route/traffic studies. Not proof of roadworthiness or autonomous-driving readiness.

8 · Agriculture & geospatial

Evaluating

  • QGIS — geospatial analysis.
  • OpenStreetMap — map data; observe ODbL attribution and share-alike obligations.
  • OpenCV and PyTorch — candidate foundations for crop/orchard image analysis.

Potential relevance to Coconut Harvest AI and the five active products. Confirm image/data rights, geographic accuracy and field conditions.

9 · CAD, electronics & prototyping

Evaluating

  • OpenSCAD — script-based 3D CAD.
  • KiCad — electronic design automation.

Use for concept development and design documentation. Physical prototypes require separate engineering review, component provenance and safety testing.

10 · Testing, security & reproducibility

Selected candidates

  • pytest — automated tests.
  • pre-commit — repeatable code checks.
  • OpenSSF Scorecard — repository security posture signals.
  • GitHub Actions — CI workflows; respect usage limits and avoid paid runners/services.

Use synthetic tests, fault injection, dependency/licence review, evidence logs and recovery procedures. A security score is not a security guarantee.

How the library maps to PUI89

  • Shared AI Core / PTI: AI foundations, multimodal perception, edge efficiency, testing and simulation.
  • Coconut Harvest AI: vision, annotation, dataset quality and agriculture/geospatial evidence.
  • AI HOME & AI NOMAD: robotics middleware, simulation, perception and edge inference.
  • AI Global SUV & AI Step-Through Scooter: mobility simulation, perception and advisory-only hazard intelligence.

This mapping indicates potential relevance, not completed integration or validated product capability.

PUI89 adoption and safety gate

  1. Verify the exact project, release, software licence, model-weight licence, dataset rights, provider terms and origin/provenance.
  2. Check privacy, telemetry, network requirements, data handling and commercial-use restrictions.
  3. Check local RAM, storage, CPU/GPU/NPU compatibility and power before any installation or download. No paid service, subscription, metered API or cloud GPU is assumed.
  4. Prefer existing dependencies, small CPU baselines and offline/reproducible workflows.
  5. Separate proposed, implemented, simulation-tested, bench-tested, field-tested and independently validated claims.
  6. For physical systems, simulation and AI recommendations never replace independent engineering, human oversight or product safety validation.

Engineering loop: define need → gather rights-cleared evidence → build a baseline → test uncertainty and failure cases → simulate where appropriate → prototype only after review → validate in controlled real-world conditions → publish evidence.
