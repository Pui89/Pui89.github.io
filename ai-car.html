PUI89 AI Car — Vehicle Intelligence Concept

A human-driver-first AI car concept for hazard awareness, road-context analysis and vehicle-health support. Software-first and simulation-led.

Open GitHub repository ↗Back to main website
Scope and status: Current status: concept and software foundation only. No road-tested prototype, live sensor integration or safety certification is claimed.

360° context awareness

A proposed architecture for combining suitable cameras and sensors with source, timestamp, quality and uncertainty tracking.

Road-risk analysis

Simulation scenarios for wet or low-grip surfaces, obstacles, reduced visibility and missing or stale observations.

Driver assistance

Advisory information only. The human driver retains control and responsibility; no autonomous actuation is enabled.

Vehicle health and cyber resilience

A design pathway for system-health checks, data-integrity controls and secure interfaces. No live vehicle connection is claimed.

Evidence-led development path

01 · Data contracts and tests

Define Observation, WorldState, ActionProposal and FailureReport contracts; test malformed and degraded inputs.

02 · Simulation

Build repeatable synthetic scenarios before considering any hardware integration.

03 · Safety review

Review hazards, failure modes, uncertainty and human approval boundaries.

04 · Evidence before claims

Publish only reproducible evidence and distinguish design intent from demonstrated capability.
